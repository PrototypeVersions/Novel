# Esse — people and relationships
Provisional biography beyond drafted scenes is planning, not yet established fact.

## Mara Ward
See foundation. Her practical goal is stable income and housing; her intellectual goal is to resume work that matters. The investigation threatens both and offers a route back to recognition. She must learn to distinguish being correct, being trusted, and having authority to decide.

## Ruth Bell, 67
Former catering supervisor, now does occasional alterations. Manages much household administration but does not speak for Leon automatically. Has deteriorating vision that limits driving; Leon's hip limits walking. Their mutual care is specific and reciprocal. She dislikes being treated as either helpless or inspirational. Wants the payment, privacy, and a reliable account of what their consent permitted. Can withdraw cooperation. Her understanding of consent and household consequences can be sharper than Mara's.

## Leon Bell, 71
Former payroll clerk. Hip operation, slower mobility, sense of humor without constant quips. Participated in the study for $80 and because he thought better planning would help. Wants to keep driving to their community garden plot and Ruth's appointments. Some of his wishes conflict: privacy, public correction, no further use of his data, no destruction of useful research. The model based on him may diverge from him. He is not a passive proxy for it.

## Daniel Mercer, 49
Episcopal priest, divorced, adult daughter. Runs Saint Anne's alongside volunteers who sometimes oppose him. Believes care is required before usefulness is established; cannot afford unlimited care. Wants the parish to survive without making attendance or belief the price of help. Skilled at organization, bad at delegating unpleasant financial news. Can seek an unfair concession for his own institution. Faith includes worship, discipline, habit, doubt and community, not simply arguments about souls. He should sometimes be impatient, mistaken, or funny.

## Ivo Marin, 38
Owns the repair business below Mara's rented rooms; leases the building rather than owning it. Former lover, now a friend with unsettled boundaries. Diagnoses physical equipment, keeps commitments to customers, dances badly without embarrassment. Has a daughter, Ana, aged ten, who stays at weekends. Will help Mara but resists becoming unpaid infrastructure for her projects. His supplier debts and lease renewal matter independently. Broad technological optimism survives because he sees what the tools let people repair.

## Amina Sayegh, 43
Former Mere evaluation lead; now teaches part-time and consults. Helped author an internal objection to treating requests for cessation as mere contamination. Also signed a limited pilot when safeguards seemed sufficient. Has no master file resolving the case. Wants an external review with access to the actual deployment configuration; fears that publicity will erase distinctions and destroy the best evidence. Her caution is partly justified and partly protective of her reputation. Has a teenage son and a sister with whom she shares care for their father. Her family and cultural life must become particular in later scenes, not shorthand for a religious position.

## Elias Venn, 36
Still employed at Mere, doing integration work. Former colleague of Mara. Helps locate public specifications and internal escalation routes but initially will not leak records. Wants defects corrected without collapsing the program. Anxious, capable, occasionally patronizing. His knowledge of the system is genuinely local.

## Leila Grant, 46
Allegheny County Council member and former care-worker organizer. To enter after the pilot. Wants enforceable resident and worker power over procurement. Political skill includes compromise and sometimes concealment. Funding and election constraints do not excuse every choice. She will challenge Mara's assumption that a technically better answer automatically deserves implementation.

## Martin Vale, 52
Mere service director. To enter later. Defends deployment because it improved waiting times and prevented some hospital admissions. Financial and contractual pressures also matter. He must be capable of telling the truth against his own interest, and capable of harm through choices he can explain.

## Tomas Reed, 29
Infrastructure technician and activist, introduced later through an established relationship rather than arriving as a convenient hacker. Wants to force an independent review by interrupting a dependency. Understands some risks and misses others. Has practical political demands; is not merely destructive or secretly an AI puppet.

## Relationship pressure

Mara/Leon: technical representation versus ownership of a personal history.
Mara/Ivo: affection, money, use of shared space, unequal expectations.
Mara/Amina: intellectual recognition, distrust, mutual correction.
Mara/Daniel: practical cooperation despite disagreement over authority and obligation.
Mara/Elias: a shared past and different estimates of what is worth risking.
